I am a security researcher and technologist based in Bangalore, India.

I have a dual focus across technology and policy. My research interests include network and application security, privacy, and Internet measurement.

Previously, I was a Senior Information Controls Fellow with the [Open Technology Fund](https://opentech.fund), hosted by the [Internet Governance Project](https://www.internetgovernance.org/2025/01/09/dns-based-web-censorship-in-india/) at the School of Public Policy, Georgia Institute of Technology, where I studied the [scale of DNS censorship in India](https://dnsblocks.in/assets/report.pdf). My past affiliations include OpenUK, [Hasgeek](https://hasgeek.com), [Kloudle](https://kloudle.com/), Stackmate, [Centre for Internet and Society (CIS India)](https://cis-india.org), and [Infosec Clinic](https://infosec.clinic/), among others.
